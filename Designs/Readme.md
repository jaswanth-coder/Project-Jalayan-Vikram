## Components List

### Parts

- **2 x N20 Motor (3V, 50 RPM)**  
  These compact motors provide reliable power for the robot's movement and propulsion.
  
- **25mm 5V Fan**  
  Helps in cooling key electronic components, ensuring the system runs smoothly during extended operations.

- **TP4056 BMS (Battery Management System)**  
  Provides charging and protection for the 18650 batteries.

- **2 x Red LED**  
  Simple indicator LEDs for signaling or status feedback.

- **Heat Sink for Camera**  
  Keeps the onboard camera cool, ensuring it functions optimally during operation.

- **Heat Sink for Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 Sense**  
  Essential for thermal management of the microcontroller.

- **DRV8833 Motor Controller**  
  Controls the two N20 motors, allowing smooth and precise movements.

- **Slide ON-OFF Switch**  
  Used to power the robot on and off with ease.

- **Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 Sense**  
  The brain of the robot, handling sensor data and communication tasks.

- **2 x 18650 Battery**  
  These provide the power supply. Batteries from an old laptop can be repurposed for this project.

- **12 x CSK Allen M3 x 10mm**  
  These screws are used for assembling the chassis and attaching components.

- **2 x Socket Button Head Cap Screw M3 x 20mm**  
  Larger screws for securing key structural components.

- **Heat Sink Compound**  
  Thermal paste to improve heat dissipation from components like the microcontroller and camera.

- **Connecting Wires Kit**  
  A set of wires to connect various electrical components.

- **Kapton Tape**  
  Heat-resistant tape to protect sensitive electronics.

---

### Tools

- **Allen Key**  
  Used for tightening and assembling the robot components with screws.

- **Sandpaper**  
  For smoothing 3D-printed parts and improving fit and finish.

- **3D Printer**  
  Used to print the custom-designed parts of the robot's chassis and other components.

- **Soldering Kit**  
  Essential for soldering connections and securing wires to the electronic components.

- **Glue Gun**  
  Helpful for quick fixes and securing components in place.
